SEO audit.

What ctrc.com does today, checked on Nov 19, 2026, and what this refresh does instead. Speed and Core Web Vitals weren't measured yet; that comes with a Lighthouse run on the live build.

IssueImpactTodayIn the refresh
Jobs are invisible to Google's job searchHighNo JobPosting structured data anywhere, and jobs live on a separate careers subdomain that showed zero postings when checked.Every job is its own page with JobPosting data: title, pay, location, shift, credentials, dates. 1,500 pages in this mockup.
Search is a separate siteHighcareers.ctrc.com runs on a different system with its own look; the main site sends people away to search.Search is the first thing on the home page, reads plain words, and updates as you type.
No local pages for the four officesHighOffices are listed on one contact page. No location markup, so maps and local results get little to work with.A page per office with EmploymentAgency markup, plus 49 town pages and 171 trade-by-town pages.
Weak page headingsMediumHeadings render as h3 and h4; most pages have no clear h1.One h1 per page that says exactly what the page is.
Duplicate content on every pageMediumThe same four testimonials and three news teasers repeat across home, about, contact, marine and welding pages.Each page carries its own content; shared blocks link out instead of repeating.
Organization data is thinMediumOrganization schema has no phone, address, founding date or social profiles.Full EmploymentAgency record: legal name, 1987, HQ address, phone, email, social profiles, and all four offices.
Name and age don't agreeMedium"CTR Group" and "CTR Corporation" are used interchangeably; the careers footer says "Over 35 Years" (1987 makes it 39).CTR Group as the brand everywhere, CTR Corporation as the legal name in the footer and schema. Years computed, never typed.
ImagesLowBlog thumbnails have empty alt text; photos are served as PNG.Vector line drawings for trades (a few hundred bytes each). Photography, when added, goes in as AVIF/WebP with alt text.
Navigation labels driftLow"Blog" vs "Blogs", "Pipefitters" vs "Pipe Fitters".One vocabulary file drives the nav, the search and the pages.
MobileLowDesktop-first WordPress theme; search and apply are separate hops.Built phone-first and installable as an app: offline saved jobs, bottom tab bar, apply in 60 seconds.

What ships in this build.

Job pages
1,500 static pages, each with JobPosting and BreadcrumbList data
Trade pages
24 trade hubs and 171 trade-by-town pages (only where 3 or more jobs exist, so none are thin)
Location pages
49 town pages; the four office pages carry EmploymentAgency data
Site search
WebSite SearchAction pointing at the home page query bar (?q=)
Sitemap
sitemap.xml with every page and its last change date
Mockup safety
This preview is marked noindex so sample jobs never reach Google. The production build flips it to index and points canonicals at www.ctrc.com.
