      02How it works

      From search to the gate in three steps.

      No forms to fight with. Your phone does the work, and a real recruiter picks up when you call.

      1. 01

        Say what you want.

        Type it the way you'd tell a recruiter: trade, town, shift, pay. The list changes as you type, and every word you used shows up as a filter you can tap off.

      2. 02

        Apply in a minute.

        Sign in with LinkedIn, a passkey or just your email. Your trade card, certs and work history go with every application, so you only fill it out once.

      3. 03

        Meet your recruiter.

        Do your screening on a video call from your phone, sign your paperwork online, and show up on day one with your badge and orientation already handled.
