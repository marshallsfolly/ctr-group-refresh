Tank Coatings Painter, 1st Class

Hiring fastNorth Kingstown, RIReq. CTR-31540Posted 10 days ago12 openings

A commercial ship repair yard in North Kingstown needs 12 tank coatings painters for container ship repair. Night shift (3rd), 6×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 20 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Abrasive blast steel to the surface profile on the work spec
  • Mask, prep and clean up between coats
  • Set up and maintain blast pots, hoses and spray rigs
  • Work in supplied air and full PPE in confined spaces
  • Spray, brush and roll coatings in tanks, voids and on the hull
  • Take wet and dry film thickness readings and log them

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $155/day per diem
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • Help finding housing near the yard
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
