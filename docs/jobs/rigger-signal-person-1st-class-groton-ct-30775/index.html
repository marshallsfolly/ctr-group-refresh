Rigger / Signal Person, 1st Class

OpenGroton, CTReq. CTR-30775Posted Oct 304 openings

We're staffing rigger / signal persons for carrier maintenance availability at a naval ship repair contractor in Groton, CT. Night shift (3rd), 5×8s, mon to fri, with a shift differential. You start on CTR's payroll. After 120 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Signal cranes by hand and radio
  • Work out loads and pick the right gear
  • Plan and rig lifts with slings, shackles and chain falls
  • Inspect rigging gear before each use

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $145/day per diem
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
