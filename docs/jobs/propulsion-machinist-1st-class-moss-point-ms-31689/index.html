Propulsion Machinist, 1st Class

OpenMoss Point, MSReq. CTR-31689Posted 9 days ago2 openings

A barge and tug builder in Moss Point needs 2 propulsion machinists for commercial vessel repair. Swing shift (2nd), 5×8s, mon to fri, with a shift differential. You start on CTR's payroll. After 90 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Work from technical manuals and job orders
  • Overhaul valves and hydraulic components aboard ship
  • Take readings with dial indicators, mics and feeler gauges
  • Pull and reinstall shafts, couplings and bearings
  • Install, align and repair pumps, motors, valves and deck machinery

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
