Lead Bulkhead Insulator

OpenBath, MEReq. CTR-31171Posted 13 days ago6 openings

A naval shipbuilder in Bath needs 6 lead bulkhead insulators for surface ship repair. Day shift, 5×10s. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Contract runs about 5 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Insulate piping, valves and bulkheads to the insulation drawing
  • Install fire insulation in machinery spaces
  • Work overhead and in tight spaces
  • Cut and fit fiberglass, mineral wool and lagging cloth

02What you need

  • 5 or more years, 2 leading a crew
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $120/day per diem
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
