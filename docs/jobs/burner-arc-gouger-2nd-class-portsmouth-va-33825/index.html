Burner / Arc Gouger, 2nd Class

OpenPortsmouth, VAReq. CTR-33825Posted Yesterday3 openings

We're staffing burner / arc gougers for barge and tug new construction at a barge and tug builder in Portsmouth, VA. Day shift, 5×8s, mon to fri. Overtime comes up most weeks. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Set up and maintain torches, hoses and regulators
  • Follow cut lines, templates and marked work
  • Arc gouge out welds and defects
  • Cut and bevel plate and shapes with oxy-fuel and plasma

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
