Shipfitter (Unit Assembly), 2nd Class

OpenSan Diego, CAReq. CTR-31521Posted 10 days ago1 opening

Join a 25-person shipfitter crew on commercial vessel repair at a commercial ship repair yard in San Diego. Night shift (3rd), 6×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 20 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Bring steel to line with jacks, strongbacks, wedges and come-alongs
  • Lay out, fit and tack plate, stiffeners and brackets from prints
  • Work in tanks, voids and double bottoms
  • Check fit-up with levels, squares and a transit before welding
  • Burn and grind to trim steel to fit
  • Install foundations, inserts and penetrations aboard ship

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • Relocation assistance
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
