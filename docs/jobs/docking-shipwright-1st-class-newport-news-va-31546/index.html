Docking Shipwright, 1st Class

OpenNewport News, VAReq. CTR-31546Posted 10 days ago4 openings

We're staffing docking shipwrights for submarine hull and outfitting work at a submarine module facility in Newport News, VA. Swing shift (2nd), 5×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Contract runs about 4 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Install and remove temporary access
  • Build and set keel and bilge blocks from the docking plan
  • Dock and undock vessels and set shores
  • Rig and move heavy timber and blocking

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $80/day per diem for those living 50+ miles out
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
