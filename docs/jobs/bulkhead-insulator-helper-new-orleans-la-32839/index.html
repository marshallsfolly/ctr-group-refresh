Bulkhead Insulator Helper

OpenNew Orleans, LAReq. CTR-32839Posted 3 days ago4 openings

We're staffing bulkhead insulator helpers for ferry and workboat construction at a barge and tug builder in New Orleans, LA. Day shift, 6×10s. Expect 20 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. You start on CTR's payroll. After 90 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Cut and fit fiberglass, mineral wool and lagging cloth
  • Work overhead and in tight spaces
  • Insulate piping, valves and bulkheads to the insulation drawing
  • Install fire insulation in machinery spaces

02What you need

  • No experience needed
  • Willing to learn the trade and show up every day
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $115/day per diem
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
