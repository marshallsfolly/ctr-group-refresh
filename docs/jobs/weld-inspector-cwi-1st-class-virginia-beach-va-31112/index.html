Weld Inspector (CWI), 1st Class

Hiring fastVirginia Beach, VAReq. CTR-31112Posted Nov 54 openings

We're staffing weld inspector (cwi)s for ferry and workboat construction at a dry dock contractor in Virginia Beach, VA. Swing shift (2nd), 5×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Short-term work, about 4 weeks.

01What you'll do

  • Perform UT, MT, PT and VT and record the results
  • Inspect welds, fit-up and dimensions against drawings and specs
  • Work with production to clear defects quickly
  • Review weld maps and NDT reports
  • Write and close nonconformance reports

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • AWS Certified Welding Inspector
  • NAVSEA Technical Publication 271 NDT certification
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
