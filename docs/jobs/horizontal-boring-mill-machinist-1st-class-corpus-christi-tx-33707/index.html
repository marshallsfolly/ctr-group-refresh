Horizontal Boring Mill Machinist, 1st Class

OpenCorpus Christi, TXReq. CTR-33707Posted Yesterday2 openings

A refinery turnaround contractor in Corpus Christi needs 2 horizontal boring mill machinists for a petrochemical plant outage. Day shift, 5×8s, mon to fri. Contract runs about 5 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Measure with mics, bore gauges and height gauges
  • Machine shafting, valve parts and fittings to tight tolerances
  • Keep machines maintained and the shop clean
  • Set up and run manual and CNC lathes, mills and boring mills
  • Read prints with GD&T callouts

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $100/day per diem
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
