Outfitting Shipfitter Helper

OpenGroton, CTReq. CTR-30867Posted Nov 124 openings

We're staffing outfitting shipfitter helpers for submarine new construction at a submarine module facility in Groton, CT. Day shift, 5×10s. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. You start on CTR's payroll. After 120 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Bring steel to line with jacks, strongbacks, wedges and come-alongs
  • Check fit-up with levels, squares and a transit before welding
  • Lay out, fit and tack plate, stiffeners and brackets from prints
  • Install foundations, inserts and penetrations aboard ship
  • Burn and grind to trim steel to fit
  • Work in tanks, voids and double bottoms

02What you need

  • No experience needed
  • Willing to learn the trade and show up every day
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $130/day per diem
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • Help finding housing near the yard
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
