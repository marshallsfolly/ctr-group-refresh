Shop Pipefitter Foreman

OpenWilliamsburg, VAReq. CTR-33305Posted 2 days ago1 opening

We're staffing shop pipefitter foremans for ferry and workboat construction at a barge and tug builder in Williamsburg, VA. Swing shift (2nd), 5×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Contract runs about 7 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Cut, bevel, fit and tack pipe, flanges and fittings
  • Hydro and pneumatic test systems and chase down leaks
  • Install hangers, supports and valves to the drawing
  • Work with copper-nickel, carbon and stainless pipe

02What you need

  • 5 or more years, 2 leading a crew
  • NCCER craft credential
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • OSHA 30-hour card
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
