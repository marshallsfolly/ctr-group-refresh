Aluminum Welder Helper

OpenSan Diego, CAReq. CTR-32406Posted 5 days ago1 opening

One opening on barge and tug new construction at a dry dock contractor in San Diego. The yard is ramping up and wants people who show up. Day shift, 5×10s. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Read weld symbols and prints and work to the weld procedure (WPS)
  • Preheat, back-gouge and grind joints to sound metal
  • Tack and close out units and blocks in the assembly hall
  • Coordinate hot work with fire watch and keep your area fire-safe
  • Repair defects called out by QA and pass UT and MT inspection
  • Weld aboard ship in tanks, voids and machinery spaces

02What you need

  • No experience needed
  • Willing to learn the trade and show up every day
  • NAVSEA Technical Publication 248 welding qualification
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • Pass a CTR weld test at the start (we pay for test time)
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
