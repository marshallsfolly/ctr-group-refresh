Portal Crane Operator, 1st Class

OpenNewport News, VAReq. CTR-31746Posted 9 days ago5 openings

A naval ship repair contractor in Newport News needs 5 portal crane operators for aircraft carrier overhaul. Night shift (3rd), 5×8s, mon to fri, with a shift differential. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Work with riggers and signal persons on critical lifts
  • Read load charts and plan lifts with the lift supervisor
  • Do pre-shift inspections and keep the crane log
  • Run portal, gantry or mobile cranes for module and material lifts

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • NCCCO crane operator certification
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Shift differential
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
