Marine Insulator, 2nd Class

Hiring fastHonolulu, HIReq. CTR-30530Posted Oct 195 openings

5 openings on ferry and workboat construction at a commercial ship repair yard in Honolulu. The yard is ramping up and wants people who show up. Swing shift (2nd), 4×10s, with a shift differential. You start on CTR's payroll. After 90 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Cut and fit fiberglass, mineral wool and lagging cloth
  • Insulate piping, valves and bulkheads to the insulation drawing
  • Work overhead and in tight spaces
  • Install fire insulation in machinery spaces

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $225/day per diem
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
