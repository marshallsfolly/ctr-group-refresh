Visual Weld Inspector, 1st Class

Hiring fastPascagoula, MSReq. CTR-34020Posted Today5 openings

Join a 36-person qc crew on container ship repair at a barge and tug builder in Pascagoula. Day shift, 4×10s. Overtime comes up most weeks. You start on CTR's payroll. After 120 days of good work the yard plans to hire on.

01What you'll do

  • Write and close nonconformance reports
  • Work with production to clear defects quickly
  • Inspect welds, fit-up and dimensions against drawings and specs
  • Perform UT, MT, PT and VT and record the results
  • Review weld maps and NDT reports

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • ASNT SNT-TC-1A Level II
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Medical, dental and vision after 60 days
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
