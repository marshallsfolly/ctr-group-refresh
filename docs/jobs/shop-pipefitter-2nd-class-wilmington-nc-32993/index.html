Shop Pipefitter, 2nd Class

OpenWilmington, NCReq. CTR-32993Posted 3 days ago15 openings

15 openings on barge and tug new construction at a steel fabrication shop in Wilmington. Good foremen, clean yard, steady hours. Day shift, 4×10s. Direct hire with the yard. CTR runs the search and gets you through onboarding.

01What you'll do

  • Fabricate and install piping systems from isometric drawings
  • Install hangers, supports and valves to the drawing
  • Cut, bevel, fit and tack pipe, flanges and fittings
  • Hydro and pneumatic test systems and chase down leaks
  • Work with copper-nickel, carbon and stainless pipe
  • Rig and move pipe spools in tight shipboard spaces

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $95/day per diem
  • Full benefits from day one, 401(k) with match
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
