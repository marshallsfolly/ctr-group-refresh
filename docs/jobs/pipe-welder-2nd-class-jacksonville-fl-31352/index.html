Pipe Welder, 2nd Class

OpenJacksonville, FLReq. CTR-31352Posted 11 days ago14 openings

Join a 23-person welder crew on surface ship repair at a naval ship repair contractor in Jacksonville. Night shift (3rd), 6×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 20 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Contract runs about 3 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Coordinate hot work with fire watch and keep your area fire-safe
  • Weld structural steel and plate in all positions with FCAW and SMAW
  • Repair defects called out by QA and pass UT and MT inspection
  • Preheat, back-gouge and grind joints to sound metal

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • NAVSEA Technical Publication 248 welding qualification
  • Pass a CTR weld test at the start (we pay for test time)
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $105/day per diem
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • Help finding housing near the yard
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
