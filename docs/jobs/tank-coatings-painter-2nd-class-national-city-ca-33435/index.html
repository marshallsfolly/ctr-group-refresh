Tank Coatings Painter, 2nd Class

Hiring fastNational City, CAReq. CTR-33435Posted 2 days ago1 opening

One opening on aircraft carrier overhaul at a naval ship repair contractor in National City. Good foremen, clean yard, steady hours. Day shift, 5×8s, mon to fri. Contract runs about 11 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Mask, prep and clean up between coats
  • Take wet and dry film thickness readings and log them
  • Work in supplied air and full PPE in confined spaces
  • Spray, brush and roll coatings in tanks, voids and on the hull
  • Set up and maintain blast pots, hoses and spray rigs
  • Abrasive blast steel to the surface profile on the work spec

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • Confined space entry training
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • $95/day per diem for those living 50+ miles out
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
