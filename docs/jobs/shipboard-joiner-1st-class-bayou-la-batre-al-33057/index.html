Shipboard Joiner, 1st Class

OpenBayou La Batre, ALReq. CTR-33057Posted 3 days ago6 openings

A commercial ship repair yard in Bayou La Batre needs 6 shipboard joiners for container ship repair. Night shift (3rd), 5×8s, mon to fri, with a shift differential. Contract runs about 4 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Install joiner bulkheads, doors and ceilings
  • Install berthing furniture, lockers and shelving
  • Lay deck covering and underlayment
  • Read joiner drawings and arrangements

02What you need

  • 3 or more years in the trade
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Your own hand tools per the tool list
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
