Communications Electronics Technician, 2nd Class

OpenMobile, ALReq. CTR-33919Posted Today3 openings

A naval shipbuilder in Mobile needs 3 communications electronics technicians for carrier maintenance availability. Swing shift (2nd), 5×10s, with a shift differential. Expect 10 hours of overtime a week at time and a half. Contract runs about 4 months, and extensions are common.

01What you'll do

  • Install and test combat, radar and communications equipment
  • Document tests and results for the customer
  • Troubleshoot to the board level with meters and scopes
  • Terminate fiber optic and coax cable to spec

02What you need

  • 1 to 3 years in the trade
  • U.S. citizenship for base access
  • TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential)
  • OSHA 10-hour card
  • Able to climb, crawl, lift 50 lb and work at heights and in confined spaces
  • Pass a drug screen and background check
  • Steel-toe boots. CTR provides the rest of your PPE.

03What you get

  • Weekly pay by direct deposit
  • Overtime at time and a half
  • Shift differential
  • Medical, dental and vision available
  • A recruiter who picks up the phone
